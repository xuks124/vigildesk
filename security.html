Security & Compliance ｜ 安全与合规

安全与合规说明：数据本地、无遥测默认关、免责声明。英文为主，每节末尾附中文要点。

1. Your data stays local ｜ 数据本地

  • All configuration, logs, decision records and reports are stored on your own machine under %LOCALAPPDATA%\VigilDesk. We do not operate a copy of your trading data.
  • Order review runs against your own local or self-hosted inference endpoint (default 127.0.0.1:8080) — order details never need to leave your VPS unless you point the endpoint elsewhere.
  • Broker credentials belong to your MT5 terminal. VigilDesk reads market and account state through the terminal on your behalf and does not ask for your broker password.

中文要点：配置、日志、决策记录与报表全部保存在本机 %LOCALAPPDATA%\VigilDesk，我们不存副本；订单审查走你自有的本地 推理端点（默认 127.0.0.1:8080），订单细节无需离开你的机器； 券商账户密码属于 MT5 终端，VigilDesk 不索取。

2. Telemetry is opt-in ｜ 无遥测，默认关

  • Watchdog Lite (free) has no telemetry at all — no license check, no reporting, no phone-home. This boundary is enforced in code and guarded by automated tests.
  • In paid editions, telemetry (crash/diagnostic reporting) is off by default and only sends when you explicitly enable it in Settings → Diagnostics.
  • The license check contacts the activation server over HTTPS when you activate or renew, and supports a 72-hour offline grace period for unattended VPS use. Nothing else is transmitted.
  • Optional webhook alerts go only to the endpoint you configure.

中文要点：免费版 Lite 完全无遥测（代码与测试双重固化该边界）； 付费版遥测默认关闭、需手动开启；激活/续期通过 HTTPS 联系授权服务器，离线宽限 72 小时；webhook 告警只发往你自行配置的地址。

3. What VigilDesk is not ｜ 免责声明

  • VigilDesk is a risk-control safety layer over your own strategy. It is not investment advice and does not generate signals of its own.
  • We never promise any profit or returns. Anyone claiming guaranteed returns "with VigilDesk" is misrepresenting the product.
  • Trading foreign exchange and CFDs on margin carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors; you could lose more than your deposit. Past performance of any strategy does not guarantee future results.
  • Test in dry-run mode first, and only use capital you can afford to risk. See the full text in legal/DISCLAIMER.txt.
