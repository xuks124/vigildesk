Verifiable proof · not a promise

We do not sell signals.
We sell a floor.

VigilDesk is a risk guard that sits next to whatever strategy you already trade. It caps what a single trade can cost you, caps what a single day can cost you, and stops you trading when you are tilted. This page proves the one claim we actually make — losses stay bounded — using the raw deal history of a real account, including the parts that do not flatter us.

3. The part that does not flatter us

Our $50 daily-loss rule was silently doing nothing for most of this account's life. We found out, fixed it, and we are showing you the evidence — because a vendor who hides this is exactly the vendor you should not trust with your account.

When we first wrote this page we intended to claim "the $50 daily cap was never breached." Then we checked. It is false, in two ways:

  1. The $50 rule was not the cap that was actually running. Code unconditionally overwrote the rule value with balance × 1.5% — roughly $142 — so the configured $50 was discarded and the breaker never fired once in the entire run.
  2. 5 of 42 trading days closed worse than −$50, and the worst day closed at $-100.26 — about 1.00% of the deposit.

And here is the receipt, from our own source code — this comment was written on 2026-10-02 when the bug was found:

# 2026-10-02 修复：原为【无条件覆盖】经验库固定值 → rule_003 配的 max_loss=50 被静默丢弃。 (line 2473)
# 这正是 rule_003 hits=0 的原因：实际帽 = 余额×1.5% ≈ $142，而单日最大亏损仅 $100.26，永不触发。 (line 2474)
# 改为取【更保守者】：规则只能收紧、不能放松（与工作区约束"约束只许收紧不许放松"一致）。 (line 2475)

Translated: before 2026-10-02 the effective cap was balance × 1.5% ≈ $142, while the worst single day was $100.26 — so the breaker never triggered, and the rule recorded zero hits. Note that the $100.26 figure was produced independently by our own code, and by the MT5 analysis behind this page — two sources, same number.

What changed: the cap is now min(balance × 1.5%, rule value), so a rule can only ever make the guard stricter, never looser (line 2479). The rule value was tightened to $100 on 2026-10-08. This is a real, dated, checkable fix — not a promise.

Why our original figure was wrong: we had been quoting a $50 cap that the code was not enforcing, and we had not reconciled it against the deal history. We are also now publishing the caliper, below, so the same mistake cannot hide again.

6. What we are actually selling

Our own research on this account says something uncomfortable and useful: we could not find predictable edge in direction. Three independent analyses of the same data put classic technical indicators at a discriminative power of roughly 0.49 — indistinguishable from a coin toss. Factor work on this instrument produced nothing that survived out-of-sample rolling validation.

So we stopped selling the thing we could not prove. The loss bound, unlike the profit, is reproducible. You can see it in the numbers above, and you can re-derive it yourself from the same raw data. That is the entire product: a floor under the bad day, and an audit log that survives a reboot.

Our commitment, in one line: we will never publish a return, a win rate, or a profit claim on this page. If you ever see one here, it is a bug — or a fraud — and you should treat everything else we have said accordingly.
