Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install VigilDesk?

1) Download the installer from the home page and verify the file size. 2) Right-click → Run as administrator. 3) Follow the first-run wizard. If your antivirus blocks the installer, whitelist it temporarily. If installation still fails, send us the logs under %LOCALAPPDATA%\VigilDesk\logs.

How do I activate my license or unbind a machine?

Paste your license key into the wizard's activation step. Your plan limits how many machines run VigilDesk at the same time. To move machines, unbind the old instance in the customer dashboard, then activate on the new one — the slot frees immediately.

VigilDesk can't find my MT5 terminal. Where is it?

VigilDesk needs terminal64.exe. Common locations:

  • C:\Program Files\MetaTrader 5\terminal64.exe (standard install)
  • Broker-branded folders, e.g. C:\Program Files\<BrokerName> MT5\

Or browse to it in the first-run wizard. Portable MT5 installs: pick the folder containing terminal64.exe.

The AI model download is slow / the CDN is lagging.

The bundled model is large. Downloads auto-resume — just restart VigilDesk if interrupted. If the CDN is slow in your region, switch the mirror in Settings → Inference, or point VigilDesk at your own OpenAI-compatible local endpoint (e.g. llama-server / ollama).

Firewall / connection problems

VigilDesk talks to your local AI endpoint (default port 8080) and optionally to the license/telemetry servers over HTTPS. Allow VigilDesk and the inference server through Windows Defender Firewall and your VPS provider's security group. Everything can run loopback-only — no inbound ports need to be opened.

Common errors
  • "terminal not found" — fix the path in Settings → MT5.
  • License expired — renew; live guarding pauses after the 72h offline grace period.
  • Model failed to load — check the inference endpoint is running and reachable on 127.0.0.1:8080.
  • Symbol not found (e.g. XAUUSDm) — brokers name symbols differently; set trading.symbol to your broker's exact id.
What is the trial / refund policy?

7-day free trial with all features, no card required. Full refund within 14 days of purchase if the product doesn't work as described — reply to your order email. Cancel subscriptions any time; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

Does VigilDesk promise profits?

No. VigilDesk is a safety layer over your own strategy: it caps per-trade losses, enforces daily drawdown limits, and reviews orders with an AI confirmer. It is not investment advice, it does not generate signals of its own, and it never promises returns. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.

What is the difference between Lite (free) and Pro? ｜ 免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？

Lite is free forever and ships Watchdog Lite only: process guard, crash auto-restart with cooldown and exponential backoff, and basic alerts (structured log + optional webhook). It has no license check, no telemetry, and no AI confirmer. Pro adds the full risk-control workstation: AI risk-control gate, per-trade loss caps, daily drawdown guards, Prop-Firm mode, report export, and priority support — on up to 5 terminals. See the full comparison on the Pricing page.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan? ｜ 可以随时升级、降级或取消吗？

Yes, any time. Upgrades take effect immediately (you pay the difference, prorated). Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid period — your features stay active until then. Your data and settings are kept, so if you come back within a reasonable time, you resume where you left off. Starting on free Lite and upgrading later requires no migration: the same installer unlocks more features after activation.
